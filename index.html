Clinical quality & safety

Hospital protocols, at your bedside.

Every Trinity visit follows the same four standards, whether it's a five-minute injection or a two-hour drip.

01

Class-B autoclave sterilisation

Surgical instruments sterilised in a Class-B vertical autoclave at 121 °C (15 psi), plus single-use sterile kits.

02

Prescription first

IV drips, Factor VIII, antibiotics and catheters need a written prescription.

03

Baseline vitals

BP, pulse and SpO2 are checked before any treatment begins, with random blood sugar for diabetic patients.

04

Safe waste disposal

Sharps and clinical waste leave with the nurse. Nothing goes in your dustbin.
